\chapter{核心配表字段示例（节选）}
\label{app:config}

\textbf{Character 表}：\texttt{id}、\texttt{prefabPath}、\texttt{bodyPrefabPath}、\texttt{hp}、\texttt{maxHp}、\texttt{roleType}。

\textbf{Bullet 表}：\texttt{id}、\texttt{prefabPath}、\texttt{duration}、\texttt{speed}、\texttt{damage}、\texttt{penetration}。

\textbf{SkillEffect 表}：\texttt{id}、\texttt{effect}、\texttt{damage}、\texttt{params}、\texttt{iconPath}。

完整 JSON 见项目 \texttt{docs/config/} 目录。
